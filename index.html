01

Introduction & Purpose

Welcome to EliteWord ("the Application", "we", "our", or "us"), developed and published by EliteSoft23. EliteWord is engineered to help language learners, students, and competitive exam candidates (such as IELTS, GRE, BCS, UPSC, SAT, and Higher Studies) master English vocabulary through scientifically proven spaced repetition, interactive lockscreen flashcards, and Android home-screen AppWidgets.

We firmly believe that education tools should strictly respect and preserve learner privacy. This Privacy Policy informs users regarding our strict policies with the collection, use, disclosure, and protection of information when using the EliteWord Android app available on the Google Play Store.

02

Information Collection & Offline-First Storage

EliteWord is intentionally architected as a completely offline-first application.

  • No Personal Identifiable Information (PII): We do not ask for or collect your personal name, email address, password, phone number, physical address, or national identifiers. No account registration or sign-in is required to use all features of EliteWord.
  • No Cloud Data Transmission: The application does not send your study records, bookmarks, quiz scores, or custom word lists to any remote server or external database.
  • Local Database Engine: All vocabulary entries, flashcard review intervals (repetitions, ease factors, interval days), focus sessions, active day streaks, and user-created custom decks are stored securely on your device’s local sandboxed SQLite storage powered by Drift.

Privacy Guarantee

Your learning history and personal flashcards belong solely to you. If you uninstall the app or clear app data via Android Settings, all locally stored study progress and custom words are completely deleted from the device.

03

Android System Permissions & Justification

In accordance with Google Play Developer Policy requirements, EliteWord requests only the minimum set of permissions necessary to deliver its core passive learning features (Lockscreen Live Wallpaper and Home-Screen AppWidgets). Below is a transparent breakdown of every permission declared in our manifest:

Permission Name Type Specific Purpose in EliteWord
SET_WALLPAPER
SET_WALLPAPER_HINTS		 Wallpaper API Allows users to set automated lockscreen or home-screen vocabulary cards using the built-in Live Wallpaper Service (VocabWallpaperService).
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Notification Used only on Android 13+ (API 33+) to dispatch scheduled local spaced repetition review reminders. No remote advertising or promotional push messages are sent.
FOREGROUND_SERVICE System Service Required by the Android operating system to power the background lockscreen wallpaper rendering service smoothly.
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED Device Lifecycle Allows Android WorkManager to re-register the periodic vocabulary rotation schedule when your smartphone restarts.
WAKE_LOCK Battery Optimization Ensures short background calculation tasks finish cleanly when updating scheduled home-screen widget words.

No Sensitive Runtime Permissions

EliteWord never requests runtime access to your Camera, Microphone, GPS Location, Contacts, SMS, Call Logs, or External Photo Gallery.

04

Text-To-Speech (TTS) & Audio Pronunciation

EliteWord provides natural audio pronunciation for vocabulary terms across app screens and home-screen widgets. This feature utilizes the device's native Android Text-To-Speech (TTS) engine (such as Google Speech Services or your phone manufacturer's default engine).

Audio generation runs locally on-device. EliteWord does not record your voice, nor does it transmit spoken audio over the internet.

05

Third-Party Services & SDKs

Unlike many mobile applications, EliteWord maintains a clean, tracker-free environment:

  • No Advertising Networks: We do not integrate Google AdMob, Unity Ads, AppLovin, or any third-party advertising SDKs.
  • No Identity Trackers: We do not integrate Facebook SDK, AppsFlyer, Adjust, or user profiling trackers.
  • No Data Brokering: We never sell, rent, monetize, or trade any user information to data brokers or marketing affiliates.
06

Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR Compliance)

EliteWord is a general audience educational vocabulary application suitable for learners of all ages, including high school and university students.

We do not knowingly collect, request, or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13 (or 16 in the European Union). Because our application does not collect personal identifiers, no child's personal data is ever recorded, stored, or processed on external servers. If a parent or guardian has questions about child safety in our educational tools, please contact us immediately.

07

Data Security & Safeguards

We value your trust in using EliteWord. Because all user progress and study data are stored exclusively in the private, sandboxed app directory of your Android device, operating system-level sandboxing prevents other applications from accessing EliteWord's local database.

In addition, built-in lexicon assets are protected using dynamic native C++ NDK machine-level validation to prevent unauthorized tampering or injection.

08

User Rights & Complete Data Deletion

Under international privacy standards (including GDPR, CCPA, and Google Play User Data policies), you retain full sovereignty over your data:

  • Right to Access & Edit: You can view, edit, or delete any custom word, deck, or study bookmark directly within the app interface at any time.
  • Right to Erase: You can instantly wipe all local data by going to Android Settings > Apps > EliteWord > Storage > Clear Storage / Clear Data, or by simply uninstalling the application.
  • No Lingering Traces: Since no server accounts exist, clearing local data or uninstalling the app permanently purges all traces of your study history.
